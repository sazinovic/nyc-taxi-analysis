## Interpretation

Observed records describe Yellow Taxi activity, not all NYC travel or unmet passenger demand. Different metrics use different selected records. Changing coverage, route/vendor mix, payment categories and analytical windows may influence trends. Monetary values are nominal. The sensitivity notebook checks January 2026 only. No causal effect or pooled multi-month median is inferred.
